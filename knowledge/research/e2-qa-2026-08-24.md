**Crash-order probe note:** The plan specifies "kill between mint and rename → recover_half_claimed completes, no unconsumed window." The `mint_and_claim` function (lifecycle.py:266-272) stamps `consumed_at` atomically within the BEGIN IMMEDIATE transaction before COMMIT. If the process dies after COMMIT but before the in-progress rename, the clearance is already consumed (no unconsumed window), and `recover_half_claimed` completes the rename. If the process dies before COMMIT, both the plan row and clearance consumption roll back — no half-state. The existing 12 `recover_half_claimed` tests in `test_lifecycle.py` all pass and verify the rename-completion path.
