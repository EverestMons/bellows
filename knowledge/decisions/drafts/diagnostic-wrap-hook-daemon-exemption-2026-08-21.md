**The two channels are NOT the same trigger — this is the correction that reframes the fix:**
- **Channel 1 (SessionStart injection) is not sentinel-gated at all.** `wrap_debt_hook.py` never reads `.wrap-in-progress`; it runs `wrap_check.py` unconditionally at every session start and injects the debt directive whenever the check exits nonzero. - **Channel 2 (Stop-lock capture) requires the sentinel** and is the damaging one: the worker cannot end its turn until **the CEO's wrap** verifies, so it acts on shared repos it was never scoped to.
