## Why this exists

The sketch's premise is that every mechanical check the cycle names already exists as bellows tooling, and that what a drafting stage would change is *who invokes them and when*. Whether that is worth building depends on a number nobody has measured: how often the Planner, running the battery by discipline, did not — or ran it after the walk it should have preceded, or read its verdict from the wrong channel. The lessons corpus carries the incidents (2026-08-03 *the one-command conformance check … never ran*; 2026-08-13 *a summary line attested a lint run that never happened*; 2026-08-14 *a fold is the only edit with no post-condition*; 2026-08-24 *a tool's verdict CHANNEL is part of its contract*). Incidents are not a rate. The registers are the population: the committed files under `eluvian-governance/governance/knowledge/research/` (P1, measured at walk 0), 2026-08-10 → 2026-09-01, schemas 0.1 → 0.3, and each one is the cycle's own record of whether the battery ran.

**What the instrument can and cannot see — say it in every count.** The matcher reads REGISTERS. A tool that ran and was not recorded looks identical to a tool that never ran; the sketch's own premise ("the register records that it happened") makes that the right unit, but the register is not the only artifact — the PLAN's Drafting Cycle block carries a Conformance line since v2.x, and Q-3b cross-checks it.
