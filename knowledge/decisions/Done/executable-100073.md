## What this changes

1. **`lifecycle.py`** — one function beside `record_step_end` (:481) and in its shape (the `try` / `_warn` / never-raise pattern): `mark_step_complete(plan_id, step_number, db_path=None) -> int` — `UPDATE steps SET status = 'complete', step_ended_at = COALESCE(step_ended_at, ?) ## Why this exists

The lifecycle DB is the record the CEO's timing table and the forge reporter read (#100058 Q3); a step that reads `awaiting_verdict` on a closed plan is a false record made by omission, and it grows by one every night a plan pauses and continues.
