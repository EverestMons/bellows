## Why this exists

D5 was adopted 2026-08-09 as the instrument that makes the drafting cycle's convergence measurable across plans, and **it was never built** — so specimen 1 was collected by hand, pass by pass, over an entire session. Specimen 2 should not cost that.

**Measured at authoring, and it scopes the tool:** the `Done/` corpus holds **63** plans carrying a `## Drafting Cycle` block; **2** of them carry an origin split (`diagnostic-322`, `executable-332`); **0** cycles have closed under v2.0, which is the version that makes the split mandatory. Control: 55 files match `Weak spots:`.

⚠️⚠️ **The claim must be narrowed, because the tool cannot see specimen 1 and will not see every successor.** Specimen 1's cycle ran on a plan that was **stopped before deposit** and lives in `governance/knowledge/research/`, never in `Done/`. **D5's collector is therefore blind to the very specimen it was built to stop hand-collecting** — and blind to any future cycle that halts or is stopped, which is exactly the population where the most instructive cycles sit (334, 328). **What this tool actually buys is: every cycle that CLOSES flows in automatically.** That is worth building and it is less than "specimen 2 will not cost a session."

**So this is a COLLECTOR, not an analyser.** The cross-plan convergence analysis D5 eventually wants needs at least three v2.0 closes; there are none. **Building that analysis now would compute a trend from two points.** What is buildable today, and worth building today, is the parse — so every v2.0 cycle flows in automatically instead of being excavated later.

⚠️ **This is the PARSE layer, not the reconciliation.** The funnel doc names close-count reconciliation — *"a warn-first check that the Cycle Log's per-lens counts reconcile against the per-phase commit trail"* — as a FORWARD candidate and the record investment that matters. **This tool does not do that**; it reads the log and nothing else. It is the layer such a check would sit on. Stated so nobody reads a shipped collector as a shipped reconciliation.

⚠️ **The record this tool reads is measurably unreliable, and the tool must not launder it.** Close-commit fold counts were wrong **4-for-4** across measured cycles (311: 30-vs-16; 317: 21-vs-14; 315: 9-vs-8; 320: absent). This tool reports **what the record claims**, labelled as such. It is not a measurement of what happened; it is a measurement of what was written down. Every output column says so.
